Advancing Longitudinal Research

Beyond Periodic Surveys: Capturing Health Experiences as Life Unfolds

Traditional surveys provide valuable structured assessments at scheduled intervals. LifeTimeline complements these methods with low-burden, in-the-moment data capture, enabling richer longitudinal insights without forcing participants to spend energy trying to remember past weeks.

calendar_month
Established Research Approach

Periodic Retrospective Surveys

  • check_circleStructured Follow-Ups: Standardized assessments conducted every 6 months to 1 year.
  • check_circleComprehensive Questionnaires: Detailed evaluations covering multiple health dimensions.
  • check_circleRetrospective Reporting: Trying to remember past weeks and experiences between visits, risking memory fatigue.
  • check_circleResearcher-Guided Interviews: Scheduled interactions support systematic data collection.
Tested Protocol
touch_app
The LifeTimeline Advancement

Low-Burden Mobile Event Timeline

  • check_circleIn-The-Moment Capture: Record health events and experiences as they happen in under 60 seconds.
  • check_circleMinimal Participant Burden: Quick, flexible interactions that fit naturally into daily life.
  • check_circleRicher Temporal Context: Captures when events occur, eliminating memory fatigue or forgetting over time.
  • check_circleParticipant-Controlled Disclosure: Participants could choose which sensitive experiences can be discussed during follow-ups.

UNCOMPROMISING PARTICIPANT PROTECTION

Your data cannot be seized, shared, or sold. Period.

In an era of digital surveillance, health research requires an absolute iron-clad shield. We deploy dual-layered legal and technical safeguards to keep you safe.

gavel

NIH Certificate of Confidentiality

Issued by the United States Department of Health & Human Services under 42 U.S.C. § 241(d). Your responses are legally immune from state and federal subpoenas, law enforcement requests, insurance auditors, and civil lawsuits.

enhanced_encryption

Private & Anonymous by Design

Personal identifying identifiers (names, phone numbers, payout emails) are physically separated on isolated servers from all observational health entries. Researchers only ever analyze anonymized, non-linkable ID sequences.

vpn_key

Irrevocable Right of Erasure

You maintain full bodily and digital ownership. You may pause, redact past entries, export your raw logs, or withdraw completely at any point with a single tap, without forfeiture of any previously earned gift cards.
